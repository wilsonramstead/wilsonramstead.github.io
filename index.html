For local owners

Why your business needs a website.

If you run a real business but you're relying on a Facebook page or a directory listing, you're leaving calls on the table. Here's the plain version.

01

They Google you first

Customers look you up before they call. No website — or a broken one — and they quietly scroll to the competitor who has a good one. The site is the handshake before the handshake.

02

Your Google link is your first impression

That website link on your Google listing is often the very first thing people click. So many are expired, broken, or missing entirely. Send me your business name and I'll check yours for free.

03

Fast, mobile, one tap to call

Most of your visitors are on a phone with low patience. A site that loads instantly and puts your number one tap away turns a curious look into a booked job.

04

You own it

Not a page trapped inside someone else's platform that can change the rules or disappear. Your domain, your site, your rules — an asset that works for you around the clock.
